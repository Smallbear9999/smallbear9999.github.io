Lana Poullos’ Diary
The cafe brunch June 23rd, 2059

I think I stopped existing this morning. Or maybe I started? I genuinely can’t tell which.

I woke up and the layers were just gone. No pings. No overlays. No dancing sunbeams that always used to slip through my curtains at exactly the right angle. Just... raw light. Still. Honest. I panicked obviously. I even unplugged and replugged the charger like that was going to fix anything.

My brunch group was meeting at the vintage-core rain café. A Luma-only space, with weather synced audio and jazz ghosts that flickered around the corners. Without the layers, I would look like a glitch, or even worse… A sympathiser. So I faked it.

I walked through the streets, smiling at blanks walls, waving at empty air trying her hardest to mimic the animations and soundscapes she had always known. At the café, I think I laughed a second too late. Maybe I stared abit too hard. Described an “aesthetic fog” until one of my friends asked which filter I was running. “That’s weird, I can’t see any fog?”

My heart almost leapt out of my chest. Sheepishly I waved it off as an older layer. “I am using Vintageloop-6” and my heart was veritably thumping as I bluffed my way through the meal. From the looks on my friends faces and the quick scribble into what I can only assume was a notes app they bought it.

-L

I work for the glasses. And I left them on my dresser? June 24th, 2059

Today, I did the impossible. I went to work for Luma. Without Luma...

The irony isn’t lost on me. I sat in that sterilised glass box and stared at screens filled with filtered visions while I wore none. I nodded at colleagues who couldn’t even see the real me beneath their lenses.

But no one noticed. Not really. Maddison asked if I was feeling "low-poly" or whatever that means but ohter than that? Nothing.

I walked home past a mural I’d never seen before. Like it was actually painted. Not projected. Tucked into an alley that no body ever dares enter. It had a girl who was crouched with her hands on her knees, eyes blacked out with bold strokes, and the words “WE SEE IN THE DARK” above.

I stopped and something cracked in me.

I feel like an imposter. I sell the dream and sleep without it.

And yet… I think I laughed today. Not a sync-laugh. A real laugh. An. Actual. Laugh. It sounded too loud. It echoed. It was ugly and awkward and mine.

I think I want more of that?

Even if it means becoming invisible to the world I helped build.

-L

I found them. June 25th, 2059

I clicked a link I wasn’t meant to click.

Buried inside an old thread buried by some ex-layer fashion account. A weird symbol. No title. Just a line of text:

“If your eyes ache, it’s because they remember how to see.”

I followed it. Tunnel after tunnel. Proxy IDs. Glitchy sites dressed up like broken marketplaces from the 2020s.

And then I found them.

Not just rumours on niche forums. Real people. Real networked people. They call themselves the Brightlight.

It’s not a cult.. I don’t think... it’s more like a refuge? They trade in raw vision. Broadcast actual footage of streets layered only with shadow and time. There was a clip of a boy watching birds. Actual birds. No wings made of light. Just feathers. Clumsy.. and eautiful.

They even have meetups, not online. Offline. No digital traces. You get a message one hour before, and you show up or you don’t. No digital hand-holding. No synced vibes. Just weather. Just breath. Just... presence?

Someone on the forum called me a “Half-Light.” Said I’m not gone enough to be free, but not layered enough to be blind. They weren’t wrong.

I’m scared. But not of them. I’m scared of what I might become without Luma.

And even more scared of what I might become with it.

Tomorrow I think I will reply to the forum.

I think I’ve started falling out of sync.

-L
